About QR Master Pro

QR Master Pro is a simple and free online QR code generator designed to help users create QR codes quickly and easily.

What can you create?

You can create QR codes for websites, text, phone numbers, email addresses and Wi-Fi networks.

Easy to Use

QR Master Pro is designed for both mobile phones and computers. You can customize your QR code and download it as an image.

Privacy Friendly

QR generation is performed in your browser. The information you enter into the generator is not intentionally stored by QR Master Pro.
