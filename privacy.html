Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to QR Master Pro. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the QR code generation process simple and transparent.

2. Information You Enter

QR Master Pro allows you to enter information such as website URLs, text, phone numbers, email details and Wi-Fi information to create QR codes.

QR generation is performed in your web browser. QR Master Pro does not intentionally store the information entered into the QR generator on its own servers.

3. Downloaded QR Codes

When you download a QR code, the generated image is saved by your browser or device. QR Master Pro does not intentionally receive or store the downloaded image.

5. Cookies

6. Advertising

If advertisements are displayed on QR Master Pro, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable laws and their own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

QR Master Pro is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through the QR generator.

8. Changes to This Policy
